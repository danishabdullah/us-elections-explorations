# Product

## Register

brand (Read mode: a data explainer, the visitor understands something)

## Users
People the author shares the page with: politically engaged readers looking at a claim about 2026 Senate results. They read it once, on a laptop or a phone, and need the finding without working it out.

## Product Purpose
Test the claim behind a viral chart (Sanders-endorsed Senate candidates vs. Harris 2024 margin) with verified, cited vote counts, and show what fairer comparisons say.

## Brand Personality
Dark, analytical, exact. Confident about what the data shows and plain about what it can't.

## Anti-references
Generic dashboards: cards, small grey labels, legends repeated per chart, titles that name the axes instead of stating the finding.

## Design Principles
- Every chart title states its finding; the chart proves it.
- Annotate on the chart; don't make the reader decode a legend.
- Show uncertainty and sample size next to the claim, not in a footnote.
- Every number traces to a cited source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast; identity never by color alone (labels, bold names); keyboard-reachable tooltips; a full data table; reduced motion respected.
